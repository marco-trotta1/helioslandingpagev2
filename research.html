When should a model change an estimate?

Research on when a model correction should be used, and when the original estimate should stay.

Published research

  1. Preprint · Marco Trotta · arXiv:2609.35314

    When Should a Satellite Estimate Be Changed? Stress-Testing Neural Corrections for Evapotranspiration

    Satellite estimates can help explain water use. A model can adjust those estimates, but an adjustment can also add error. This paper studies how to check for evidence before using a change.

    Read the preprint ↗

What this means for Helios

This work highlights a simple principle: recommendations should rely on evidence that fits the decision. The paper studies satellite estimates of water use. It is separate research and does not describe a live Helios feature.

Helios, in plain language

Helios helps growers decide when to irrigate and how much water to apply. It brings field readings, crop needs, and weather into one clear recommendation. Growers make the final call.
